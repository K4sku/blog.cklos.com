WARDOGS mortar calculator

Paste your coordinates, paste the target's, read range and heading.

—
  1. Open the map, zoom in on your mortar, right-click → Mark coordinates, Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C, paste into the first field.
  2. Do the same on the target and paste it into the second field.
  3. Middle-click the target on the map, turn to the heading above (the diamond should be centred), set RNG to the range, fire.
